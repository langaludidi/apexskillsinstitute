2. Scope

This Policy applies to information processed through the Apex website, course enquiries, applications, enrolments, learning platforms, assessments, payments, organisation engagements, events, communications, the Faculty Network and other Apex activities.

3. Information we may collect

Depending on the relationship, Apex may process name, contact details, location, identification information where legitimately required, education and qualification information, employment and professional information, programme selections, application and enrolment information, assessment and learning records, certification records, communications, payment and transaction records, organisation information, technical website information and other information voluntarily supplied.

4. Learner information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, process applications, manage enrolment, provide learning, administer assessments, issue certificates, provide learner support, administer payments, maintain appropriate academic and administrative records and communicate about relevant programmes.

5. Faculty Network information

Faculty applicants may provide name, contact information, location, professional profile, employment history, qualifications, professional registrations, expertise, sector and facilitation experience, delivery preferences, languages, geographic availability, CV or professional profile, supporting professional documents and rate expectations where requested. Initial applications should not require unnecessary banking, tax or identity-document information; such information may be requested later only where reasonably required for approved contractor onboarding.

6. How we collect information

Information may be collected directly from you, through website forms, enrolment, learning platforms, assessments, correspondence, organisations legitimately arranging training, Faculty Network applications, authorised service providers and lawful public or professional sources where appropriate.

7. Why we process personal information

Apex may process information to provide requested services, process applications and enrolments, manage learning and assessment, communicate with learners, administer certificates and transactions, respond to enquiries, manage organisation engagements, evaluate and manage Faculty Network applicants, identify suitable facilitators and subject experts, maintain security, meet legal obligations, protect legitimate interests, improve services and conduct marketing where lawfully permitted.

8. Faculty Network purpose limitation

Faculty information is primarily used to evaluate suitability, verify information where appropriate, categorise expertise, contact applicants, manage the faculty pipeline, identify potential programme assignments and manage subsequent professional engagements. Submission does not guarantee appointment or work.

9. Marketing

Apex manages direct electronic marketing in accordance with applicable law. Faculty Network consent does not automatically constitute marketing consent. Any marketing opt-in is separate, optional and not preselected.

10. Sharing information

Where reasonably necessary, information may be shared with authorised employees, facilitators or administrators, technology and learning-platform providers, payment providers, hosting providers, professional advisers, assessment or quality-assurance providers, contracted operators and authorities where disclosure is legally required. Apex does not sell personal information as a commercial database.

11. Operators and service providers

Where another organisation processes information on behalf of Apex, Apex seeks to use appropriate providers and contractual and security arrangements having regard to applicable legal requirements.

12. Cross-border processing

Some technology providers may store or process information outside South Africa. Apex will take reasonable steps to ensure transfers and processing are handled consistently with applicable data-protection requirements.

13. Security

Apex implements reasonable organisational and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of information processed. No information system can be guaranteed completely secure. Security compromises involving personal information will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

14. Retention

Apex retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the collection purpose, legitimate operational or academic recordkeeping, contractual requirements, legal obligations, dispute management and other lawful purposes. Different information categories may have different retention periods. Unsuccessful Faculty Network applications will not automatically be retained indefinitely.

15. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or destruction where appropriate, objection to certain processing, withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent, direct-marketing preferences and complaints.

Requests should be directed to Langa Ludidi, Information Officer at chairman@apexskillsinstitute.co.za. Apex may require sufficient information to verify identity and locate the relevant record.

16. Cookies and website technology

Apex may use cookies and similar technologies for essential operation, security, preferences, analytics, performance and other lawful website purposes. Appropriate notices or choices will be presented where required.

17. Children and minors

Apex does not intentionally process children’s personal information without an appropriate lawful basis and any required consent or authorisation. Programmes intended for minors require appropriate additional safeguards.

18. Third-party websites

Third-party websites and platforms have their own privacy practices. Apex is not responsible for independently operated third parties’ privacy practices.
