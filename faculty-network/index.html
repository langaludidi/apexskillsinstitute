Apex Faculty Network

Bring real-world expertise into practical learning

Apex Skills Institute is building a national network of experienced facilitators, practitioners, subject-matter experts and industry professionals who can contribute to practical, relevant and professionally delivered learning.

Expertise that strengthens learning

Apex’s learning philosophy is built around application. We look beyond academic knowledge alone. Our Faculty Network brings together people who understand their disciplines and the environments in which that knowledge must be applied.

You do not have to be a traditional lecturer

Some of the strongest learning comes from people who have actually done the work. Apex welcomes experienced professionals, managers, practitioners, consultants, public officials, entrepreneurs and technical specialists who can contribute meaningfully to applied learning.
